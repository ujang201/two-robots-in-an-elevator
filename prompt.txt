Write one funny line of awkward small talk that one robot says to another
while they ride an elevator together, under 200 characters.

The line must:
- read as a single line of small talk between two robots in an elevator;
- sound like a machine talking, with robot logic, firmware, sensors or
  charging habits showing through the politeness;
- land the awkwardness: the pause, the forced courtesy, the thing neither
  robot wants to name;
- be funny on the first read, with a turn that surprises rather than a
  familiar pun;
- stay tight, with every word earning its place.

Avoid generic elevator jokes and weather puns. Aim for a specific,
surprising machine-logic twist on the silence itself.
